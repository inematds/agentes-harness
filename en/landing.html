Who it’s for

Made for decision-makers, not programmers

it's for you to

  • You run a department, an office, a clinic, or your own business, and want to know what AI actually does beyond answering questions.
  • You already use ChatGPT or Claude in the browser and feel that big tasks are still manual.
  • You want a clear argument for deciding where your team should work with AI.

it's not for you if

  • You already code and want to learn commands and configuration in depth (look for the Claude Code technical track on INEMA).
  • You want a course with a long video lesson: here, the video is 2 minutes, and the rest is reading and practice.
